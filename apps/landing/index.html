Bring your own host

Your machine.
Your provider accounts.
Your rules.

Run Cumea on this Mac, or keep it online on a VM you control. Cumea does not provide a managed cloud computer, and it does not proxy your credentials through a Cumea service.

Choose the host

Your desktop for on-demand work, or your authenticated VM for always-on routines.

02

Connect the providers you use

Claude, Codex, Grok and Gemini adapters share one driver contract; capabilities stay explicit.

03

Pair the companion

A short-lived, one-use secret enrolls mobile. Device tokens can be revoked from the trusted host.

Security by boundary

Private by default,
remote by choice.

The local harness stays on loopback. Remote access is a separate, opt-in listener that requires HTTPS. Mobile receives a narrowed, authenticated event stream—not provider system prompts, internal reasoning, provider-native errors, raw frames or configuration secrets.
